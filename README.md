# Agent briefs

The briefs behind the videos on [David Kahl](https://www.youtube.com/@DavidKahlBuilds): what I actually tell agents when I build things with them.

They aren't magic prompts. Everyone has access to the same models, and what makes the difference is the direction you give them: what you ask for, what you rule out, and what you check afterwards. These briefs are starting points for that. Adapt them to your own project, and keep reviewing what comes back.

## How the briefs are written

Every brief is polished for reuse. Where I found out during an episode that a brief works better with more detail, the detail is in here. That means some briefs are better than the ones used on screen. Those say so at the top:

> Refined from the version used in the video.

Each file has the brief itself (copy it, replace the `<placeholders>`), what it's for, and tips on what to watch out for.
