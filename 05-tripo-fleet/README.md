# Episode 05 · The Tripo fleet

A fleet of Tripo-generated vehicles becomes flying traffic in Unreal: different speeds, vertical overtaking, visible hover pads and camera views that show the traffic working. The vehicles can be replaced through a catalog, without rewriting the traffic system.

## The briefs

1. [Build the traffic system](01-unreal-traffic-system.md): one standalone brief for importing the fleet, building the simulation, adding the two traffic modes and checking the saved result.

This is a rewrite based on the final result and what we learned while getting there. It brings the requirements together upfront; it isn't a transcript of the prompts used during the episode. Native simulation is an option in this brief, while the episode's traffic simulation used Blueprints.

Bring your own vehicle models and Unreal environment. This repository contains the instructions, not the models, textures, marketplace environment or finished Unreal project. Replace the placeholders in the brief and choose its code policy before using it.
